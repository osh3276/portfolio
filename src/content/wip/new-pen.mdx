## context
Despite my handwriting being rather illegible, I am a sucker for a good pen. I introduced to fountain pens by my fourth grade teacher, but I didn't think much of them at the time. Years later, on a late night on Reddit during my junior year of high school, I stumbled upon the r/fountainpens subreddit. Now I have four.
